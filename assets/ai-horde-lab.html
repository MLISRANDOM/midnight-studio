Known limits

This uses independent volunteer GPUs, not paid Higgsfield models. Worker capacity, image dimensions, queue times and CORS/network access vary. If direct requests are blocked by your browser, this interface reports the error rather than pretending generation worked. Images may expire at their remote URLs.
