## Set pieces to retain
- Sheep scare: eyes and chewing in darkness; torch reveals a sheep. He is ashamed and relieved. Skeleton threat may follow after a breath; vary later scare resolutions.
- Crafting table: four planks snap into a cube; painted tools do not pull off; he quietly asks it for a pickaxe. Correct arrangement makes the tool appear, startling him. Show raw-material-to-plank crafting first. He tries to carry the table, then eventually discovers ordinary block pickup rather than hauling it forever.
- Livestock: he turns with wheat and discovers a crowd of animals following. Breeding is an awkward surprise shown without graphic content; use correct foods for chosen species.
- Bread flirt: adult female villager's apparent romantic attention follows bread, not his face. Later friendship can be real without making the joke a love story.
- Nether bed beat: he begins to place it, remembers danger and stops. No gratuitous explosion to manufacture comments.
- Ocean: he knows the potion should work but cannot make himself inhale underwater. The first successful breath is relief, then something moves beyond his light.
- Dragon: he still says he cannot do it while reaching for another arrow. Earned competence with persistent human fear.
