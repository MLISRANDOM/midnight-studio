# Original stories with reference pacing

Enter one to three original English beats in Motion Lab and select **Turn idea into shots**. A quantized MiniLM sentence encoder runs in the browser and compares the wording to six actions the rig can perform. Each selected action has a framing. For a one- or two-beat idea the planner fills the remaining shots with authored transition choices. The shot editor lets the creator override either choice. The render report records the original idea, model revision, clause matches, framing, measured reference profile, and exact frame boundaries. Scene export is silent.

For the controlled test, two different ideas used the same approved Survivor sheet and the complete measured Big Buck Bunny reference. The first produced inspect, knock and cover. The second produced listen, signal and recoil. Each encoded 193 frames of 540 × 960 AVC at 24 fps. The reference came from the checksum-verified Blender master. User-provided authorized videos can also be measured through Files or Drive. Arbitrary YouTube links depend on delivery of decodable media. Confidence scores are cosine similarities; they are not calibrated probabilities.

The planner selects authored character animation. Generating new characters, environments, arbitrary actions, dialogue, sound, or complete episodes from a prompt remains open.
