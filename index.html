Video and project export

A browser-created motion comic is genuinely exportable on supporting devices. iPhone Safari may not support canvas video recording, so export is detected rather than promised.

Video export has no soundtrack. Use the included scene narration and your preferred free editor to add voice, music, and final mix. This limitation is deliberate; recording system speech with reliable timing is not supported across iPhones.

Optional free AI artwork
